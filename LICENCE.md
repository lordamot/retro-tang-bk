  its register map, its memory model, its display, blitter and sound,
  its disk protocol and its BIOS - is MAXIOL's design, documented by
  him on forum.maxiol.com; `tang/src/bk/az*.v` are written against
  that documentation and GID's emulator, and are this repository's
  code, not his.  `soft/azbk/` is MAXIOL's card package as it ships
  beside GID's emulator: `AZ.INI`, the AZ ROMs (`azboot.ROM`,
  `AZLIB00..03.ROM`, `AZ337.ROM`, `SETUP.ROM`, the logo) and the disk
  images under `DISKS/`.  It is redistributed here so that the machine
  can run and the simulation can boot; the rights to it are MAXIOL's,
  and it is to be taken as he publishes it (`.claude/docs/tools.md`).
- **GID's BKemu** (gid.pdp-11.ru) is the model this follows for what the
  software sees (`.claude/docs/platform.md`); `tang/src/bk/az_palette.v`
  is generated by `tools/palette.py` from its palette tables, and the
  IMA decoder in `azsound.v` follows its `ima.cpp`.  `AY_TEST` on
  `DISKS/WRKANDOS2.IMG` is GID's (2012).
- `soft/azbk/ROM/11M_*.ROM`, `10_*.ROM` are the БК-0011М's and
  БК-0010's own firmware (Электроника, 1985-1990), as the package
  carries them.
- `tang/src/bk/vm1/` - the 1801VM1 model, copyright 2014-2019
  1801BM1@gmail.com (Vslav), in Sorgelig's wrapper (2016) from the
  MiSTer BK0011M core, GPL v2.  One change, marked in `vm1_plm.v`'s
  head: the microcode matrices' `casex` compares are mask-and-value
  compares.  The GPL applies to those files.
- `tang/src/bk/ym2149.sv` - MiSTer's, copyright 2005 MikeJ and
  2016-2019 Sorgelig, under its own BSD-style notice in the file.
- `tang/src/mister/`, `mnano/` - MiSTeryNano by Till Harbaum, as carried
  by UKNC Nano, PK8000 Nano, Korvet Nano and ZS-256 Nano; `mnano/u8g2`
  is olikraus's u8g2 (BSD); `tang/src/mister/sector_dpram.v` is Gowin
  IP Core Generator output.
- `tang/src/hdmi/tmds_channel.v` is derived from Sameer Puri's
  hdl-util/hdmi (MIT), rewritten; the coding tables are the HDMI
  standard's.
- `tools/macro11` (fetched by `tools/fetch.sh`, not committed) is
